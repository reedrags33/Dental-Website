What we treat

We have quality treatment options for any stage of your dental journey.

Every visit starts with listening, ensuring the plan we build fits your mouth, your schedule, and your goals, not a one-size-fits-all checklist.

Cleanings & preventive care

Regular cleanings and digital X-rays that catch small problems in their earliest, easiest-to-treat stage.

Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings that restore your tooth while blending with your smile.

Crowns

A custom-fitted crown to repair larger cavities or fractured teeth; built to last and match your smile.

Implant restorations

Implant-supported crowns designed to look, feel, and chew like the tooth you are used to.

Emergency dental care

A cracked tooth or sudden toothache doesn't wait for a convenient time. We offer prompt appointments when you need them most.

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening that brightens your smile safely, on a timeline that works for you.

Cosmetic dentistry

Small changes, or bigger ones, to achieve the smile you have been waiting for.

Removable prosthesis

Dentures and partials fitted for comfort and a natural look, letting you eat, speak, and smile with confidence again.

TMJ & facial pain therapy

We have a multi-disciplinary approach to jaw pain that treats the issue at the source.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us what is bothering you, and we will help you figure out the right first visit.
